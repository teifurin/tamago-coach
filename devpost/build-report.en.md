# Tamago Coach: build report (English summary)

Summary of `build-report.md` (the full report, in Chinese) for reviewers. Built 2026-11-07 for the Devpost **Learn AR with SPECS** hackathon from the approved `design-brief.md`.

## Pipeline

1. **Plan.** The Devpost Learn skill pack's `plan-lens` interviewed the author (seven questions, about thirty minutes) and wrote `design-brief.md`: one spatial moment, every element placed in centimeters, five done criteria that can be checked in the Lens Studio preview.
2. **Build.** Claude Code, with Snap's CLAD plugin (`ls-clad`) and the Lens Studio MCP, built the Lens from the brief in one pass, then applied every later change as a design note. No scene edits were made by hand.
3. **Test.** LEAF (Lens Studio's automation framework, package 2.0.2) drove the Lens with simulated SIK interactors: seven scenarios, serial, in the preview.

Lens Studio 5.24.1, SIK 2.0.0, UI Kit. The project was never signed in; the build itself made zero requests to Snap services.

## What CLAD built

- **Geometry, all code.** Counter, tamagoyaki pan (rounded inner corners, silver rim, two-tone handle), grab bar, timer ring, egg layer, rolled log, six slices and the roll arrow are MeshBuilder primitives generated by `Assets/Scripts/Meshes/*.ts`. No generative 3D, no asset-library models.
- **Sound.** The chime (`Assets/GeneratedSFX/ChimeSoft.wav`) was synthesized locally.
- **UI.** Step card, Next/Back buttons and the Demo ×10 switch use Spectacles UI Kit; the ring is a SIK Interactable; the grab bar carries SIK InteractableManipulation on the set's root.
- **Logic.** Thirteen TypeScript components around a step state machine (`TamagoCoachMain.ts`): six steps, auto-starting timers, collapse/expand of the card, the roll cue, the egg's readiness cue and growth, placement and billboarding.
- **Wiring.** Scene hierarchy and script inputs were applied through the MCP's VirtualScene tool; compiles, runtime logs and preview screenshots went through MCP tools as well.

## Verification loop

After each change: recompile through MCP, read the runtime log, capture the Preview panel from the user's viewpoint (head 45 cm above and 70 cm in front of the counter, looking down about 33°), judge the capture, fix, repeat.

## LEAF results (final full rerun after all changes, 7/7 pass)

| Scenario | Checks | Result |
|---|---|---|
| tc-01 start layout | Set 70.0 cm ahead, 45.0 cm below eye level; card beside the pan's far rim; no drift over 2 s | pass |
| tc-02 steps | Next walks Prep → Heat → Layer 1 → Layer 2 → Layer 3 → Rest & slice; Back returns one step | pass |
| tc-03 countdown and pause | Ring counts down (59.6 s → 56.5 s after 3 s); pinching the ring freezes it; pinching again resumes | pass |
| tc-04 roll cue | With Demo ×10 on, Layer 1 reaches zero in about 2.7 s: card says "Roll now", arrow enabled; after Next the arrow hides and the log grows 0 → 2.6 cm | pass |
| tc-05 grab and drag | Dragging the bar moves pan, ring, card and buttons together (8.5 cm, 0.00 cm off-axis); nothing drifts after release | pass |
| tc-06 IK reach | A full-arm IK interactor reaches Next; Next is 76 cm from the head, so it is pinched through the hand ray rather than touched | pass |
| tc-07 Demo switch regression | `setDemoSpeed(true)` makes `isDemoSpeed()` true and the switch show "on"; `false` reverses it | pass |

Two first-round failures, both fixed:
- tc-04 failed with `isDemoSpeed() after setDemoSpeed(true) Expected: "true" - Received: "false"`. Real bug: the UI Kit Switch callback receives an "explicit" flag, not the on/off state. Fixed by reading `sw.isOn`; tc-07 now locks it.
- tc-05 failed with `off-axis motion 68.91 cm, expected <= 3`. The simulated interactor reported a ray distance of zero, so SIK clamped the dragged set to 1 cm. The test now reports a 500 cm ray like a real hand; the Lens was not changed.

Not covered by tests: walking around with WASD, text readability, the chime (checked by ear), the look of the six slices.

## Design changes after the build (by design note)

1. The first rolled log (2.6 cm) hid behind the 3 cm near wall of the pan from the 33° look-down. The log now sits at the far side of the pan, which also matches the real technique (push the roll to the far side before the next pour).
2. Next and Back sat 83 cm from the head. Moved to the near side of the counter (76 cm). Still a ray pinch; labeled "feel unproven" for real glasses.
3. Demo switch bug, above.

## Visual polish round

Milestone 1 was a grey-box prototype. Round one of polish, again through CLAD with design notes and still all code-generated geometry: Uber PBR materials with vertex color (wood counter with noise and bevel; graphite pan with a silver rim; egg that goes from glossy and slightly translucent to matte as the countdown runs; banded rolled log), a warm key light and environment reflections, contact shadows, a low-saturation capsule grab bar with grip rings, a 0.8 cm thick sweeping arrow with fading trails, a UI Kit rounded card with a "Step N · Title" header and accent line, styled Next/Back buttons and Demo switch. The raised card then overflowed the SPECS vertical field of view by about 1°, so the ring was raised only 1.5 cm and the card tilted to 55°: the card top now sits 17.0° above the gaze center, inside the 18.6° half field. All seven LEAF scenarios were rerun after the polish: 7/7.

Screenshots: `screenshots/polish1-prep.png`, `polish1-countdown.png`, `polish1-rollnow.png`, `polish1-done.png`.

Round two added kitchen context and feedback, again with no colliders or interactables on the new objects and everything parented to the stove set: two faint wisps of steam over the pan while the Heat countdown runs (soft elongated sprites, under 30% opacity, rising 9 cm and fading), a small ceramic bowl with beaten egg and a pair of 22 cm wooden chopsticks resting on its rim at the far-right corner of the counter, the six slices appearing one by one 0.15 s apart at Done with a single 0.6 s warm pulse of the ring and the card's accent line, and a 512 px Lens icon (`icon.png`) drawn with Pillow. The icon could not be set persistently through the Editor API (`metaInfo.setIcon` reverts), so it is kept as a file for the Devpost submission. LEAF was rerun a third time after round two: 7/7.

Screenshots: `screenshots/polish2-prep.png`, `polish2-heat-steam.png`, `polish2-done-pulse.png`.

## Demo video

The footage is the Lens Studio preview, captured with ffmpeg from the Preview panel while three extra LEAF scenarios drove the Lens: `DemoTourScenario` walks the six steps with the pauses the video needs, `DemoGrabScenario` drags the grab bar, and `DemoCameraScenario` moves the preview camera for the reveal and the walk-around. They have no assertions and are registered in `LeafIndex.ts` next to the seven tests. The narration is text-to-speech (Gemini TTS); the chime in the video is the Lens's own `ChimeSoft.wav` placed at the countdown zeros. No SPECS device was used.

## Known limitations

- Next and Back are pinched through the hand ray, not touched directly (76 cm from the head).
- The dark halo behind the card is invisible on the additive display; it only helps the preview and the video.
- Comfort of looking down for minutes, hand tracking with greasy hands and chime timing on the device are "feel unproven": the preview cannot show them.
